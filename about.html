About Me

Hello! My name is Kate and I am a digital artist based in New Jersey. I currently draw characters from video games and anime but occasionally I create work of my own original characters. Outside of art, I am a college student that enjoys cosplaying as well as playing video games. My main platforms for posting are Instagram and Tiktok and main platforms for communication are those two as well as Discord. If you would like to reach out about anything, please feel free to!
